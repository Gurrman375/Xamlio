Xamlio Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Xamlio ("Xamlio," "we," "us," or "our") is a media browsing and playback application for Xbox and compatible Windows devices. This Privacy Policy explains how Xamlio handles information when you use the application.

Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Xamlio may handle:

  • Stremio authentication information required to sign in and synchronize your account.
  • Email or account information provided through the Stremio login process.
  • Library items, watched and unwatched episode information, playback position, playback duration, and continue-watching data.
  • Streaming server URLs entered by you in the application.
  • Addon URLs that you install or configure.
  • Search terms and title information sent to addons or provider services to retrieve results.
  • Technical information automatically transmitted by network services, such as IP address, device information, request information, and error information.

Xamlio does not intentionally collect payment information, precise location information, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate your Stremio account.
  • Load your library and addon content.
  • Synchronize watched status and playback progress.
  • Resume media from the saved playback position.
  • Retrieve search results, metadata, subtitles, and available streams.
  • Open legal provider results through services such as WatchHub and JustWatch.
  • Connect to a streaming server that you personally configure.
  • Maintain and improve application reliability.

Third-Party Services

Xamlio connects to third-party services selected by you or required for the requested feature. These may include:

  • Stremio account and synchronization services.
  • Stremio addons installed by default or added by the user.
  • WatchHub, which helps identify where content may legally be available.
  • JustWatch, which may be opened to display legal subscription, rental, purchase, free, or ad-supported availability.
  • Streaming servers configured by the user.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Information sent to a third-party service is governed by that service's policies.

Storage

Xamlio stores certain settings and locally saved information on your device, including preferences, addon configuration, authentication state, streaming server settings, and locally cached playback information.

When account synchronization is enabled, library, watched-state, and playback information may be transmitted to Stremio's services so it can be accessed across supported devices.

Xamlio does not operate an independent advertising or data-broker service.

Sharing of Information

Xamlio does not sell personal information.

Information may be sent to Stremio, addons, streaming servers, WatchHub, JustWatch, and other services only when required to provide a feature you request or enable. Xamlio may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the security and rights of users and the application.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you remove it, clear the application data, uninstall Xamlio, or change the relevant setting.

Account-synchronized information is retained according to the policies of the associated Stremio account and services. To request deletion of information associated with Xamlio, contact us using the information below.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and any streaming server URLs you enter.

Children's Privacy

Xamlio is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the application, contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will include a revised effective date.
